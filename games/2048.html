How to start

The fastest clean route into 2048

  1. Start with the immediate objective in mind: Slide numbered tiles together and build toward the classic 2048 goal.
  2. Slide the whole board in one direction at a time so matching values combine while your highest numbers stay in a controlled corner.
  3. Pick a home corner early and commit to feeding your larger tiles back into it instead of shifting them across the board repeatedly.
