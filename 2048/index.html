Look for

Pick a home corner early and commit to feeding your larger tiles back into it instead of shifting them across the board repeatedly.

Main pressure

The classic appeal of 2048 comes from simple controls paired with meaningful layout discipline; one bad swipe can undo several careful turns.

Easy mistake

The standard error is chasing every available merge and breaking the board shape that made the higher-value chain stable in the first place.
