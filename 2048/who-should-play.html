2048 sits in the easy to medium range. That usually means the rules are readable quickly, but strong runs still depend on better sequencing, cleaner positioning, and fewer recovery mistakes.

Who usually gets the most value from it

The cleanest audience match is still simple: Anyone who wants a quick number puzzle with simple controls.

Merge games look relaxed on the surface, but they become much stronger when you manage board space deliberately.
