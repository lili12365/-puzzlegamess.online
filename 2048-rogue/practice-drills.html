Overview

What this page covers

2048 Rogue gets stronger once you stop treating each move as a one-off reaction. The real gains usually come from building upward through disciplined number management, accepting pressure and making longer-run survival decisions, and thinking a couple of turns ahead instead of solving only the current move and from keeping the run readable enough to avoid recovery chaos.
